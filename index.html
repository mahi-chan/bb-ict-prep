    How to use this

    Read the notes once, then live in the questions

    The notes exist so you never need the textbook again. The marks, though, come from the question sections. Every answer is collapsed by default — read the question, work it on paper, then open the solution and compare your steps against it, not just your final number.

    Write answers the way the solutions are written

    Given data first, with units converted up front. Then numbered steps: formula, substitution, result. Then a bold final answer. Examiners award steps, so a correct number with no working scores less than a clear derivation with a slip in it.

    The ⚑ marks are not decoration

    Items flagged ⚑ in SYLLABUS.md have already appeared in the supplied papers or are standard repeats — subnetting, Banker's algorithm, page replacement, min-heap construction, latency computation, infix to postfix. Each one has a fully worked example on its subject page.

    Everything works offline

    Open index.html directly from disk. All links are relative and no script is fetched from the network. Fonts come from Google when a connection exists and fall back to the system stack when it does not, without shifting the layout.

    Keyboard

    Press / anywhere to jump into search. Use ↑ ↓ to move through results and Enter to open one. Inside a question list, ↑ ↓ move between questions and Enter or Space opens the solution.
